# Ascon source ledger

This ledger records the sources consulted during the October 21 study. External performance figures are the source authors' results, not measurements of this repository. Algorithm versions, devices and evidence limits are listed separately. A legacy Ascon-128a schedule informs p8/p12 scheduling; neither byte compatibility nor identical complete transaction cycles follows from the round counts. Inaccessible and abstract-only sources remain leads, not verification of their methods. No exhaustive novelty claim is made.

Current status: [research report](Ascon%20weight%20stream%20bottlenecks.md), [fix note](../docs/ad_padding_fix.md), and numbered paper references generated by [fold_report_sections.py](../paper/fold_report_sections.py).
